# Level of Effort table

For funders, trustees and auditors. Roles, not people. Official hours where available.

## Header

```
Level of Effort — [programme or grant] — [period], as of [date]
Basis: Official hours (recorded hours after the workspace daily limit, frozen on lock)
Source: Hoursly time entries; [n] entries, [n] not yet locked
```

If Official is unavailable: `Basis: Recorded hours (Official hours not available from this connection)`.

Rules:

- Planned columns only when a plan exists on the server; otherwise omit them.
- A withheld role stays withheld in every column that derives from its actual.
- If exactly one role is withheld, the total row's hours may still be shown (hours are not cost), but any cost total follows the server's `restricted` flags.
- Keep "Before role tracking" as its own row when the server reports it.

## Notes block

- Any entries unapproved or unlocked at the as-of date.
- Running timers excluded.
- Overlapping entries explained.
- What the connection could not see (for example, people-level detail).

## What not to include

Names, salaries, cost rates, margin, other programmes or clients, beneficiary detail, or any figure the server withheld.
